          Visual Inspection Reference Guides

          No-Tool Checks
          • Tire Pressure & Tread GuideFront: 30–32 psi | Rear: 35 psi (normal) / 40 psi (full load). Minimum tread depth: 1.6 mm.
          • Brake Wear & Fluid GuideFront pad min: 2.0 mm | Rear shoe min: 1.5 mm. Squealing tab = replace pads; spongy pedal = bleed DOT 3/4 fluid.
          • Coolant Temp & Fan Clutch GuideNormal gauge: just below 1/2 mark (82–88°C). Rising above 1/2 in traffic = weak silicone fan clutch or clogged radiator.
          • Drive Belt & Transmission Fluid GuideV-belt deflection: 8–10 mm (no cracks/glazing). Jatco ATF should be cherry red—dark brown/burnt = drain & refill Dexron III.

          🚨 Emergency & Roadside Fallback Center

          100% offline safety guides, contacts, prep checklist & incident log for Isuzu Crosswind owners.

          CRITICAL AUTOMOTIVE SAFETY RULES (STOP & CALL ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE IF UNSAFE):
          • NEVER open a hot radiator cap—pressurized boiling coolant causes severe scalding burns.
          • NEVER work underneath a vehicle supported only by a jack—always use jack stands and wheel chocks on solid, level ground.
          • NEVER continue driving with suspected brake failure, steering loss, or a flashing red oil pressure warning light.
          • WARNING SIGNS vs. DIAGNOSIS: Dashboard lights, steam, or noises are warning signs—pull over safely first and verify exact torque/fluid specs against your model's official Isuzu Service Manual.
