# Crosswind Garage v6.0 — Complete Maintenance, Vehicle Tools & Emergency Center

Live App: **https://esp046-cyber.github.io/isuzucrosswindcarguide/**
Direct Emergency Center Anchor: **https://esp046-cyber.github.io/isuzucrosswindcarguide/#emergency**

### Key Modules (All Offline-Capable)
1. **Vehicle Status HUD & PMS Reminder System** (`#dashboard`)
2. **Maintenance & Inspection Checklists** (`#maintain`)
3. **Parts Finder & Compatibility Checker** (`#parts`)
4. **Service History, PDF Print & JSON Backup/Restore** (`#history`)
5. **Repair Cost Tracker, Fuel Economy Calculator & Resale Log** (`#costs`)
6. **Diesel Troubleshooting Guide** (`#diagnose`)
7. **Vehicle Tools Center (Battery Checker, OBD-II Codes, Fuse Map)** (`#tools`)
8. **Technical Specifications by Engine, Year & Transmission** (`#specs`)
9. **Emergency & Roadside Fallback Center** (`#emergency`) — 7 searchable/expandable safety guides, Emergency Contact Directory with one-tap calling, interactive Trunk Emergency Prep Checklist, and Offline Incident Notes Log.
